Privacy Policy

Simply Balance for Android · Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version

Everything you log — food, weight, water, goals and your profile — is stored on your phone. There are no accounts and no Health Connect, and we operate no server that your log is sent to.

The app uses the internet to look up a food on Open Food Facts (and, for a barcode it doesn't know, USDA FoodData Central) — the barcode you scanned, or the words you searched for — and its built-in camera readers, Google's ML Kit, send Google anonymous diagnostics about how they are working. The free version shows one ad, served by Google AdMob; buying Premium removes it. None of your log goes to any of them.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following in storage that belongs to it on your own device:

  • your food log, custom foods, recent and frequent foods, and copied meals;
  • your weigh-ins, water log and the weight trend calculated from them;
  • the profile you enter at setup — units, age, height, weight, activity level, goal and pace — and the calorie and macro targets worked out from it;
  • your settings and reminder times;
  • a local copy of foods you looked up on Open Food Facts, so the same barcode works offline next time.

The app opts out of Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer, so no copy is made on your behalf. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore it for you.

No account, name, device identifier, food log, weight or profile is sent with the Open Food Facts lookups. Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your device's IP address when the app connects to it; its handling of that is described in the Open Food Facts privacy policy. The app identifies itself to Open Food Facts as "SimplyBalance" with our support address, as their rules ask.

Apart from the ML Kit diagnostics and the free version's AdMob banner above, Simply Balance contains no analytics software. The ad never receives your food log, weight, goals or profile. The app does not read from or write to Health Connect, Google Fit or any other health service.

Permissions

  • Camera — to scan a barcode, or (Premium) a Nutrition Facts label. The picture is read on your phone to find the barcode number or the label's numbers and is not saved or sent. Optional: you can log by search without it.
  • Notifications — for the weigh-in, weekly check-in and logging reminders you choose. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us to trigger them.
  • Internet — for food lookups, the camera readers' diagnostics, the free version's ad, and Google Play purchases, all described above.
  • Advertising ID — declared by Google's ad software (free version) so ads can be measured. You can delete or limit it in Android settings at any time.

Simply Balance requests no other permissions. It has no access to your location, contacts, microphone, files or other apps' health data.

Children

Simply Balance is for adults. It is not directed to children, and setup asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any entry, weigh-in or custom food in the app at any time.
  • Use the app without the camera or without ever searching Open Food Facts; the built-in food table works offline.
  • Turn reminders off in Settings.
  • Buy Premium (Settings → Premium) to remove the ad; the ad software then never starts.
  • In the EEA and UK, change your ad consent at any time in Settings → Premium → Ad privacy options.
  • Uninstall to erase everything on the device.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request — but if you have a question, please write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how data is handled, note it in the app's release notes.
