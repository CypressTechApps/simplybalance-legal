What Simply Balance is — and is not

  • Nutrition facts can be wrong. The built-in food table comes from the USDA, and products looked up by barcode or search come from Open Food Facts, a database edited by volunteers. Either may contain errors or differ from the package in your hand. Check the label when it matters.
  • Targets are estimates. Calorie needs are worked out with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and an activity factor, and the weekly check-in adjusts them from your logged food and weight trend. Real needs vary from person to person, and the result is only as good as what you log.
  • Reminders are a convenience. Android may delay or skip notifications, for example to save battery. Do not rely on them for anything time-critical.
