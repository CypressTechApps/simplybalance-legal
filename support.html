Help & Support

Simply Balance for Android · Last updated 15 October 2026

Answers to the questions we expect most. If yours isn't here, write to us — the address is at the bottom, and a real person reads it. Please include the app version from Settings, your phone model and what happened.

Logging

What's the fastest way to log a food?

Tap + (or press and hold it for Add food). Recent and Frequent show what you eat most, and one tap logs it with your usual amount. You can undo straight away.

A barcode isn't found.

The app checks your phone first, then Open Food Facts. If neither has it, create a custom food from the label — it keeps the barcode, so the next scan finds it instantly.

The nutrition for a product looks wrong.

Open Food Facts is edited by volunteers and is sometimes wrong. Check the label, and save a custom food with the right numbers.

I forgot to log part of a day.

The app notices days that look partly logged. At the weekly check-in you can mark a day complete, add an estimate, or skip it so it doesn't skew your maintenance estimate.

Targets and check-ins

How are my calories worked out?

At first from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, your activity level and your chosen pace. After a few weeks of logging, the weekly check-in compares what you ate with your weight trend and suggests an adjustment, at most 150 kcal a week. You can accept or ignore it.

Can I set my own macros?

Yes. Goals has preset splits and three linked sliders that always add up to 100%.

Your data

Where is my log kept?

Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy.

I got a new phone. Does my log move across?

Not yet. The app opts out of Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer so your log never leaves the device without you. An export is planned.

Does it connect to Health Connect or Google Fit?

No, by design. Simply Balance keeps your health data to itself.
